
# HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition and Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition and workforce data using Power BI. The dashboard helps understand employee attrition patterns across different departments, job roles, age groups, overtime status, and tenure.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition.
- Identify departments with higher attrition.
- Analyze attrition by job role and age group.
- Understand the impact of overtime on employee attrition.
- Compare self and manager performance ratings across departments.

## 📂 Dataset Information

- *Dataset:* HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Performance
- *Source:* Kaggle
- *Total Employee Records:* 1,470
- *Number of Tables:* 5

## 💡 Key Insights

- Technology has the highest employee count (961).
- Sales has the highest department-wise attrition rate (20.63%).
- Sales Representatives have the highest job-role attrition rate (39.76%).
- Employees in the 25–34 age group show the highest attrition rate (19.80%).
- Employees working overtime have a higher attrition rate (30.53%).
- Average self rating is higher than average of manager rating across the department.

## 🎯 Business Impact

This analysis helps HR teams to:

- Identify employee attrition patterns.
- Develop effective employee retention strategies.
- Improve employee engagement.
- Review overtime and workload distribution.
- Provide better onboarding support for new employees.
- Make data-driven HR decisions.
